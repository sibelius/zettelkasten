This book is about techniques to improve your product development flow

In manufacturing you generate value creating the same product

In product development you only generate value modifying your existing product

Fullstack handles better variable demand

you can model backend and frontend engineer as processors of queues

If you have variable demands

Sometimes you gonna have much work on backend and sometime more work on frontends

In both scenario you gonna delay your tasks product development

Fullstack acts as a single poll of resources that can handle any item in the queue, causing less delays

Fast refresh is about iteration speed

Integration speed reduces defects
