Growth Loops

Standing Ovation

How to make your audience engage?

Use a question

When you use a question in your tweet, you are basically asking people (your audience) to answer it

People care about their name, their opinions and everything that they think are their own.

Most people don’t start a discussion or tweet because of the Standing Ovation model, so when tweeting give voice to them to engage and show their opinions.

Use an image/video/gif or audio

text is low resolution cold medium (Hot Cold Media)

sometimes text is not enough to conceive the information because it needs more context to make it sense of it.

Use buzz word

buzz word (hashtags) are a great way to find your content

bots can retweet you based on some hashtags

you tweet will show up when people search this hashtag

Tag some people

when you tag some people (not in the begin of the tweet - because it will restrict the tweet to only people that follow you and the other people)

your tweet will also reach their audience

Do not tag too many people, and not always the same people, avoid spamming, use with caution.

External Links

Make sure external links produces a good social image (og) to improve engagement.

Share

Make sure to share your tweet in many places (slack, discord, facebook and other)

Improve your reach

Relink and Retweet

When doing a new tweet, try to find related tweets of yours, and relink them in threads

Threads

A good thread can improve the value of individual tweets

A group of tweets has more values of all individual tweets

Funny

People like funny things

Meme

Bring image, and gifs to get a hot funny medium
