Digital Garden

  • writing
  • publishing

Updated Oct 24, 2026

Edit on GitHub

#digital #output

A digital garden is a place where I can show my content but I can also change the “engine” of it.

If you keep pushing all content websites you’re gonna hit some “limit”

It won’t be able to handle all your workflow

One way to make it possible to handle all your crazy ideas is to have access to the codebase, and own your publication

You can start simple with simple text blocks, and keep adding more blocks are your requirements changes over time.

It is a process of curate your output process
