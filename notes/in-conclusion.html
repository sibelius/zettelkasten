  1. To Summarize: Succinctly recap the main points discussed throughout the article.
  2. In Summary: Provide a brief and concise recap of the article’s content.
  3. To Conclude: Offer a closing thought or statement summarizing the key ideas.
  4. Wrapping Up: Signal that the article is concluding and summarize the main takeaways.
  5. Final Thoughts: Share your final reflections, insights, or opinions on the topic.
  6. Closing Remarks: Add some concluding comments, impressions, or opinions.
  7. To Wrap It Up: Summarize the article’s main points and bring the discussion to a close.
  8. Last Words: Offer some final insights or thoughts on the subject matter.
  9. The Takeaway: Highlight the central message or primary lessons for the reader.
  10. In Closing: Provide a concise summary and any last considerations.
  11. To End: Convey that the article is nearing its conclusion and offer a summary.
  12. To Sum Up: Recap the article’s key points in a clear and concise manner.
  13. In Summary of All This: Summarize the article’s main points and tie them together.
  14. To Recap: Offer a brief overview of the main discussion points.
  15. To Close: Signal the article’s conclusion and summarize the essential content.
