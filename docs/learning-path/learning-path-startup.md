A startup is a company searching for a product that people want, and a way to grow it.
Most die from building something nobody wants, not from bad code.

Learn it in this order: find the problem, prove it, sell it, then scale it.
Talk to customers every week, at every stage.

## 0. Foundations

- [ ] read [Paul Graham's essays](https://paulgraham.com/articles.html), start with [How to Start a Startup](https://paulgraham.com/start.html) and [Do Things that Don't Scale](https://paulgraham.com/ds.html)
- [ ] watch [YC Startup School](https://www.startupschool.org/)
- [ ] read Zero to One, The Lean Startup and The Hard Thing About Hard Things
- [ ] learn [disruption](../notes/disruption) and why incumbents ignore small markets
- [ ] learn to [handle uncertainty](../notes/hability-to-handle-uncertainty) and [risk](../notes/risk)

## 1. Find a problem

- [ ] pick a problem you understand from the inside, ideally one you have
- [ ] write down who has the problem, how they solve it today, and how much it costs them
- [ ] interview 20 potential customers without pitching. Read [The Mom Test](https://www.momtestbook.com/)
- [ ] look for the [gap](../notes/gap): what people hack together with spreadsheets and WhatsApp
- [ ] decide [what to build next](../notes/what-should-you-build-next) by pain, not by what is fun to code

## 2. Build the MVP

- [ ] build the smallest [MVP](../notes/mvp) that solves the core workflow
- [ ] ship in weeks, not months, use boring technology you already know
- [ ] do things manually behind the scenes before you automate them
- [ ] charge from day one, money is the only real validation
- [ ] learn [product development](../notes/product-development) as a flow, small batches and fast feedback

## 3. Product market fit

- [ ] measure retention: do users come back without you asking
- [ ] ask "how would you feel if you could no longer use this product?", aim for 40% very disappointed
- [ ] talk to the users who love it, find out why, and build more of that
- [ ] pivot fast when it is not working, but change one thing at a time

## 4. Sell and grow

- [ ] founders sell first: do the first 100 sales yourself before hiring sales
- [ ] write a sales playbook: who to call, what to say, what objections come up
- [ ] pick one channel and master it: outbound, content, partnerships, or product-led
- [ ] build [growth loops](../notes/growth-loops), not funnels
- [ ] build an [audience](../notes/audience) by writing in public
- [ ] learn pricing: charge for value, raise prices, and test it
- [ ] track the numbers that matter: revenue, growth rate, gross margin, CAC, LTV, burn and runway

## 5. Company and money

- [ ] open the company with a good accountant (contador): CNPJ, LTDA or S.A., tax regime (Simples Nacional, Lucro Presumido, Lucro Real)
- [ ] split equity between founders with vesting and a cliff
- [ ] learn the [Marco Legal das Startups](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/lcp/lcp182.htm), and the investment instruments used in Brazil: mútuo conversível and SAFE-like contracts
- [ ] learn how venture capital works, read Venture Deals
- [ ] bootstrap as long as you can, raise money to accelerate something that already works
- [ ] learn [blitzscaling](../notes/blitzscaling): when speed matters more than efficiency
- [ ] understand your [investing](../notes/investing) and personal runway before you quit your job

## 6. Hire and build culture

- [ ] hire slowly, fire fast, avoid the [wrong hiring strategy](../notes/wrong-hiring-strategy)
- [ ] hire people better than you, and give them ownership
- [ ] write down the [culture](../culture) before it writes itself, see [creating a culture from scratch](../notes/criar-cultura-do-zero)
- [ ] build a hiring pipeline, see the [hiring](/hiring) page
- [ ] run the company in writing: docs, async updates, decisions recorded

## 7. Operate and scale

- [ ] automate operations with code, see [StartupOps at Woovi](../notes/startupops-building-woovi)
- [ ] keep a [product vision](../notes/product-vision): vision, strategy and tactics
- [ ] set goals for the quarter and review them every week
- [ ] read High Output Management to learn how to manage managers
- [ ] learn from the [PayPal mafia](../notes/paypal-mafia): great teams spread and build more companies

## 8. Capstone

- [ ] a company with paying customers, a monthly update to investors or advisors, and one metric that grows every month
- [ ] get [startup mentorship](../notes/startup-mentorship) or [paid consulting](../paid-consulting-startups) when you are stuck
- [ ] write what you learned, and share it

## How the pieces connect

```
 problem ──► interviews ──► MVP ──► paying users ──► retention?
                ▲                                       │
                └──────────── no: change one thing ◄────┤
                                                        ▼ yes
                         sales + growth loops ──► revenue ──► hire ──► culture
                                                     │
                                                     └──► raise money to go faster
```
