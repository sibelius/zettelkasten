#design #writing #creativity

A blank canvas is starting to do something out of nothing

It is very hard to build something without nothing to start with

The idea of Zettelkasten is to fill this canvas with your preprocessed notes, so you can just reorganized them to write the final text.

You're gonna have all the sources, quotes and material to mix and match to write the final text

Don't start from a Blank Canvas
