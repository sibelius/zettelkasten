#book #blocks

Where good ideas come from is a book of how to come up with good ideas.

Good ideas is based on other ideas.

You can think of an idea is a building block for your next ideas.

Some ideas are good, but no ready yet. There are some missing pieces to make it work.

For Uber work well, we need a good mobile phone hardware with GPS, we also need a good broad brand connection.

For youtube we need the digital camera and some internet connection.

Some ideas/projects fail because there are some building blocks that are not mature anymore

- https://www.amazon.com.br/Where-Good-Ideas-Steven-Johnson/dp/1594485380

[The Nonesuch Beast](./the-nonesuch-beast) is about combination of simple requirements and low level building blocks.
