Non-coders will provide many requirements that are simple by itself, but in combination they can't exist.

Instead of trying to solve all the problems with specific features

We should provide low level building blocks ([Where good ideas come from](./where-good-ideas-come-from) , Building Block ) that let user compose to build more complex features.

Simple building blocks make it easy to learn and to use tools.

Irreducible complexity is when you can't reduce complexity of all requirements, you need to understand that you need to live with tradeoffs, and choose them based on your current requirements.
