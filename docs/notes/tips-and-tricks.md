#tips #tricks

Small tips and tricks can leverage your productive when using tools.

Try to learn tips to master your IDE, your Terminal, your text editor, your note taking app, your operational system

it sounds silly, but this is all about tiny details that matters

Most people know Undo and Redo.

A few people know how to go back and forward in navigation inside app

Notion and Webstorm uses ctrl+\[ and ctrl+\] to be able to go back and forth so you can move around and avoid losing context

Learn keyboard shortcut to move fast

Mouse is good, but it is slow as you need to navigate the "whole" screen

the keyboard goes directly to where you wanna go
