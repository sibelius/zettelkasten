#social

What would be a social Zettelkasten ?

We are social human being, we need to talk to each other for fun and to discuss our ideas

As most of our ideas are inside our [Zettelkasten Notion](./zettelkasten-notion) , if we [Open Source](./open-source) them, we could let other leverage our knowledge to build their own.

It would be an open knowledge base
