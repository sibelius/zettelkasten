Creative Thinking is when you create new fresh ideas

Over Thinking is to create ideas based on other ideas

One way to avoid blocks in creative thinking is to always do a bit of creative thinking everyday, make creative thinking a habit.

To avoid blocks try to do other types of creative thinking, instead of writing, try drawing.

Instead of drawing, trying code.

Instead of backend, try frontend.
