[Information](./information)

Text is low resolution, it let some uncertainty to participants to feel in, it brings more engagement

Audio/Video are more high resolution, it has less uncertainty.

If you are a beginner video/audio is easier to you to follow because they will have more “complete” information.

Text are better to store gists, tl;dr;

It is easier to skim and to compose.

To make read productive you need to be able to control low and high resolution reading, with and without details.

---

What is better to learn?

If you have no knowledge about this subject, try to go for videos/audio first, high resolution to fill most of your basic questions.

Then move to a low res medium to gather more knowledge faster, and fill yourself the uncertainty to [Make it stick](./make-it-stick) better.

[Workshop](./workshop) is a mix of high and low res.

Explanation and introduction to concepts uses high res videos, and exercises are low res to make your mind feel the gaps of [Reality has a lot of details](./reality-has-a-lot-of-details)
