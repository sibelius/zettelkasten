If you still have a twitter account you can use it to build and grow your audience

The audience is the people that followed you based on the content you create everyday.

The bigger your audience the bigger your reach.

You can use the audience for many reasons, make it easy to find good people to hire to your company, to sell products or courses, spread your ideas.

Time

When you post influences how many people are wake, and how many will interact/engage with your tweet

Prefer 8am to 8pm of your audience timezone. I try to overlap Brazil and US timezone.

Content

A Tweet with just a text provided fewer context and could not be understand well by everybody.

Adding images, gifs, video could improve engagement of your tweets

Links with social media preview also improve engagement

Threads helps you to provide info that won’t fit in a single tweet, or a list of things you wanna share.

It could also be a short blog.

Tagging account could also increase reach if the people tagged has a lot of followers.

Sharing

Only posting on twitter could not be enough, try to post in other communities: slack, discord, Facebook groups, WhatsApp groups, telegram and so on.

Also send to friends, and ask for retweets to improve reach
