We can separate medias in low and high resolution.

Low resolution gives you less detail and you need to fill what is missing with your own context.

High resolution gives you much more detail, so you don't have any misunderstanding

When production content, you can produce using low or high resolution.

I think the first draft of anything should be low resolution

When doing the first version of the code you should not care about all the tiny details, keep the high resolution to the end.

When doing the first design, do the first draft with "low" quality, with less details, maybe even without colors, prefer a wireframe.

When building a house you build first the structure and you finish the house wall paint color in the end

If you try to product both low and high resolution at the same time you going to struggle.

You can think of it as building your content/code/house in layers.

Each layer will have more details, and more resolution than the last one.
