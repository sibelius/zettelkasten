#business

SaaS is Software as a Service.

Instead of customer paying an unique time for a software in a box, it pays for a service and the deliver of the software in a daily/monthly basis.

Opposite to [Software House](./software-house) that deliver the software once and that's it.

it is hard to sell a digital good, as the sound industry discovered.

Users will pirate their mp3 to listen to music.

The subscription means that you're going to deliver value over time.

A subscription only makes sense if you keep delivering value over time.

A SaaS uses a subscription as a two way incentive to deliver value over time.

At Entria we started as a [Software House](./software-house) development software for big companies like Natura and Gol.

Then we transitioned to Feedback House, our SaaS product.

SaaS has a Long Term incentive, as we need to keep improving our product to sales to more users and keep current users happy without churning.
