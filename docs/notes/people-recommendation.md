Young people does not realize how they are killing their social proof

Why I can't recommend you anymore

I can't recommend you anymore because you lie

You lie that you are going to commit to that company, project, but you just disappear

I can't recommend you because you do not take responsibility for your actions, you blame others instead of saying that you did something wrong.

I can't recommend you because you don't care. You don't care about my friends.

People came to me for recommendations because they know my reputation.

They know I know a lot of good developers because I keep mentoring a lot of them.

They know that I won't recommend a bad developers (not only bad code), but bad matters.

A good reputation can open a lot of good opportunities for you

A bad reputation will close many of them.
