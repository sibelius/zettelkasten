#newsletter #writing

Newsletters have a different incentive than normal blog posts

When you do a blog post, one post is not related to another.

The likes from one blog post won't affect another one.

When you are building a newsletters, the incentive is to keep producing content in a regular basis to grow your audience (subscribers)
