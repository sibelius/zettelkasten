We have perishable problems and opportunities

A perishable problem is one that is small at first and can get big fast, so you need to handle it quickly

Example: fire, it is easier to handle small fires.

A perishable opportunity will only be available to the one that grab it first.

Example.: the first company with a covid tracking solution will win the market. The first vaccine will earn a lot of money with roalties

Decentralization can help you solve these perishable problems and to use the opportunities quickly

Escalation can help find important problems

We a mission to align decentralized resources

A mission is a declarative way of the final state

It does not matter what steps or how to achieve this final state

Make intent clear

“If you are going to worry about it, I won’t”

What is our main effort? Main effort of your product? Core features that differentiate from another
