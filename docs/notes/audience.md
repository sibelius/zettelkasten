An audience is a group of people that is listen to a talk, that will use a product.

In the same way a talk can fail when targeting a wrong audience ([A Failed Talk](./a-failed-talk) ).

A product/service will fail if it target the wrong audience.

An audience is the market of your Product Market Fit, without it you won't go further and won't succeed ([How to be Successful](./how-to-be-successful) )
