if you never feel any discomfort about your level of transparency, **you probably aren’t transparent enough**.

**You can’t trust someone’s ‘yes’ until you hear their ‘no**

As employees, they have a **right to know** about the business they are part of

**ADULTS DON’T LIKE BEING TOLD WHAT TO DO**

People _want_ to be challenged. They _want_ you to ask them to step up and take responsibility for something hard. They want to be needed, and they want to have agency in the doing of it.
