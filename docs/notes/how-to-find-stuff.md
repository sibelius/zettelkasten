#blog #search

If you keep forgetting about where you saw something, you need to improve your index to make this easy

I always forget how to find the author of zines, then I linked her twitter account inside a Zettel of my Zettelkasten, and then I can easily find her typing `zine`

I've also have some `favorite` zines that was hard to find, so I started linking them in related `zettles` to make them easier to find.

if you have something that is hard to find, it needs more connection in your web of knowledge to make retrieval process faster.

Make them searchable in different ways: tags, key words, questions, answers.

Tweak your search machine.
