As a developer you can have many types of proof of work

You can have a portfolio website that shows all your past projects (a few dots of your path)

You can have some contributions to open source projects (more dots of your path)

You can use learn in public strategy to have as many dots as possible about what went right and what went wrong in your learning path

People that can create new things are rare

You can have a CV or Linkedin (worse proof of work), as anybody can say anything on it.
