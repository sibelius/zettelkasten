Trust is when one person trust another person.

We can use [Game Theory](./game-theory) to model trust behavior among agents.

When we trust we can avoid some transactions costs.

Uber/AirBnb only works because they made trust works in the scenarios.

Uber makes safe and possible to you get inside a car of a "strange" and reach your destiny without trouble.

AirBnb let you stay in a house of a "strange" in a safe way.
