You can divide growth in 2 parts

- Acquisition loop where you get more users to your platform, and let current users spread your product using sharing strategy or word of moutn
- Engagement loop where you keep the current users engaged using notifications based on user interaction with the platform

A loop is better than a funnel, as the end of the loop will be used to increase the beginner of the strategy
